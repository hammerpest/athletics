# Long Jump: the day, from the station teacher's side

Draft for Jordan, 5 November 2026, updated with Jordan's answers to questions 51 to 57 (decisions 71 to 76). Source: `docs/2027 field event sheets/INSTRUCTIONS - Long Jump.pdf`, decisions 27, 30, 32, 33, 36, 38, 44, 57, 64 and Aths Opt 1.1 (PROJECT-STATE 2.3). It follows the same layout as `docs/high-jump-flow.md`.

Who: two teachers at one station (Long Jump 1 or 2), plus the officials the sheet names. One watches the take-off board for foot faults. One lays the tape in the sand and reads the distance. The recorder holds the iPad or iPhone. One age group, boys or girls, at a time.

"App today" is what PROJECT-STATE says is built (boards A1.8 iPad, A1.10 phone, A1.20 Take Names). It was not re-checked screen by screen. "Change?" marks a gap between the sheet and the app, or a choice for Jordan. Nothing here is built or drawn yet.

## 1. Before the athletes arrive

What the teachers do: rake the pit level, clear the runway, check the take-off board is clean and the tape is ready.

In the app:
1. Open the app, enter the Field Station PIN, pick the station (Long Jump 1 or 2).
2. The screen shows the group for this block (for example "Boys 17 Years Long Jump") and "Up to 3 jumps".

App today: station, group and 3 jumps are held as organiser data.

## 2. Athletes arrive: announce and take names

What the teachers do: announce "Boys 16 Years Long Jump". Record each name and give a number in arrival order. Tell athletes how many jumps they have.

In the app (Take Names, A1.20):
1. The recorder taps each name as the athlete arrives. They get the next number, which is their jumping order.
2. A second tap takes a name off and the numbers close up.
3. "Not on the list" adds a missing athlete.
4. The screen says each athlete has up to 3 jumps, so it can be announced.
5. Tap Start jumping.

App today: built, on iPad and phone, except the jumps line in step 4 is only a chip on the next screen.

## 3. Jumps: up to 3 for everyone, the official decides

Decision 72 (replaces decision 33 and the sheet's "2 jumps then the top 4"): every athlete can take up to 3 jumps, in the same order each round. There is no "2 jumps then top 4" button.

The official can end an athlete's jumping at any time. An athlete who only wants one jump finishes with one. When there is no time (for example 40 athletes), the official ends a number of athletes after one jump so the event finishes inside its time window.

In the app:
1. Each athlete has a Finish button. It asks first ("Finish Mark Jones with 1 jump?") and can be undone.
2. After a round, Keep top N: the official picks N, the app marks everyone below the Nth best mark as finished, and the official can still change the list before the next round.
3. A finished athlete shows their jumps so far, and their place and points count as normal.

## 4. Each round: call, jump, measure, record

What the teachers do: "Number 1, Mark Jones. Next, Andrew Johns." The foot-fault official watches the board. The tape official puts the tape on the mark closest to the board, made by any part of the body, and measures to the edge of the board nearest the pit. The recorder records while the sand is raked and the next athlete gets ready.

In the app (phone, A1.10):
1. The screen shows the athlete, "Athlete 3 of 7", their 3 jump boxes, and Next: the next name.
2. Good jump: the tape official reads the distance, the recorder types it on the keypad in metres to the nearest centimetre (decision 38), for example 5 3 4 for 5.34 m. Tap Next athlete.
3. Foul or no jump: record a foul (shown as x). A foul has no mark. The sheet's five fouls are all the same to the app: no mark.
4. Taking off before the board is not a foul. The jump is measured from the board, so the recorder types the distance as normal.
5. A wrong entry: Undo last, or tap a filled jump to change it. A later automatic change never overwrites typed work.
6. The best of the athlete's jumps is their mark. Equal best marks share the place and the points (decision 36).

In the app (iPad, A1.8): the same, typed into the grid: one row for each athlete with Jump 1, Jump 2, Jump 3, Best, Place, Points, No show.

App today: typed marks, x for a foul, - for a pass, Best, Place, Points and No show are built.
Change (decision 74): Foul and Pass buttons beside the keypad, so a foul is 1 tap and not a typed letter.

## 5. Things that happen during the event

- **Athlete does not turn up or goes home:** tap No show. Their earlier jumps stay.
- **Late athlete after Start:** Add an athlete. The official decides how many jumps they get (decision 75).
- **Re-jump after an official's ruling:** Undo last on the jump, then record the new one.
- **Wrong mark found later:** tap the jump and fix it. Places and points are worked out again.
- **Device dies or reloads:** it reopens on this station, this group, at the same athlete. Nothing typed is lost. Saves wait in a queue with no signal and show "Waiting to send" until sent.
- **Both stations run at once:** Long Jump 1 and 2 each have their own device and group.

## 6. Later rounds and cutting the field

1. Rounds 2 and 3 use the same jumping order as round 1 (decision 72). Finished athletes are skipped.
2. If time is short after any round, use Keep top N or Finish on single athletes (decision 73).
3. The event ends when every athlete has taken 3 jumps or has been finished.

## 7. The end of the event

What the teachers do: mark 1st, 2nd and 3rd on the recording sheet and send it to the recording room with an athlete.

In the app:
1. When the last jump is recorded, tap Group finished.
2. The phone shows the group results to check (the A1.22 screen, decision 76, with a column for each jump): place, name, house, best mark, all jumps, points. An athlete with no mark shows "No mark" and 0 points.
3. Check against what you saw, fix a jump with Back to jumping if needed, then tap Send results.
4. The Recorder and Announcer get it live. No paper is carried.
5. After Send the screen shows "Sent at 10:52" and Next group. With no signal it says "Waiting to send" until it has gone.

App today: Group finished, Send and the Recorder override are built. The phone results screen is drawn for high jump only (A1.22).

## 8. Athletes leave, next group arrives

1. After Send, the screen opens the next group for this station and its Take Names screen.
2. The teachers rake the pit and check the tape. Finished athletes go back to their house area.
3. At the last group of the day, the screen says this station has no more groups.
