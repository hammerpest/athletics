# Shot Put and Discus: the day, from the station teacher's side

Draft for Jordan, 5 November 2026, updated with Jordan's answers to questions 58 to 62, 64 and 65 (decisions 78 to 84). Question 63 is still open. Source: `docs/2027 field event sheets/INSTRUCTIONS - Shot Put.pdf` and `INSTRUCTIONS - Discus.pdf`, decisions 27, 30, 32, 34, 36, 38, 44, 57, 64, 72 to 76 and Aths Opt 1.1 (PROJECT-STATE 2.3). Javelin is not used at CCAS (decision 31). It follows the same layout as `docs/high-jump-flow.md` and `docs/long-jump-flow.md`.

Shot put and discus run the same way in the app, with different weights, so this is one flow. Where they differ it says so.

Who: two teachers at each station (Discus 1, Discus 2, Shot Put), plus the people the sheet names: one official watches for fouls, one marks where the shot or discus lands, and one returns it (and must watch every throw). The recorder holds the iPad or iPhone. One age group at a time, except Shot Put, which has two age groups at once in four blocks (10:40, 11:10, 11:40 and 12:10).

"App today" is what PROJECT-STATE says is built (boards A1.18 iPad, A1.19 phone, A1.20 Take Names). It was not re-checked screen by screen. "Change?" marks a gap or a choice for Jordan. Nothing here is drawn or built yet.

## 1. Before the athletes arrive

What the teachers do: keep all athletes behind the safety area. Nobody collects the shot or discus until the official says. The person returning it watches every throw. Check the right weight for the age group.

In the app:
1. Open the app, enter the Field Station PIN, pick the station (Discus 1 or 2, or Shot Put).
2. The screen shows the group (for example "Boys 17 Years Shot Put") and the weight for the age, for example "Shot: 5 kg" (decision 34), and "3 throws each".
3. The teacher checks the right implement is on the circle.

App today: station, group and weights by age and sex are held as organiser data and shown at the station.
Change? Question 63 (a safety reminder). Question 62 for Discus 12 and 13, where the weight changes by age.

## 2. Athletes arrive: announce and take names

What the teachers do: announce "Boys 16 Years Shot Put". Record each name and give a number in arrival order. This is the throwing order and the marker number. Tell athletes they have 3 throws.

In the app (Take Names, A1.20):
1. The recorder taps each name as the athlete arrives. They get the next number, which is also their marker number.
2. A second tap takes a name off and the numbers close up. "Not on the list" adds a missing athlete.
3. Tap Start throwing.

App today: built, on iPad and phone.

## 3. How a group runs (decisions 78 to 82)

Shot put and discus do not run like the long jump. Everyone gets a marker, and not everyone throws three times:

1. **Throw 1:** everyone throws once, in number order. Each athlete's marker stays where their throw landed, and they remember their marker number.
2. **Throw again:** the official looks at the markers and picks about 10 athletes (a guide, not a limit) to throw again. Those athletes take throws 2 and 3, once per round in number order.
3. **The rest** end after throw 1. They are participation athletes and are not measured.
4. **Measuring:** at the end the official measures about 5 athletes (a guide) and records a mark for each.
5. **Participation:** every athlete who is not measured gets one set distance, typed once by the official and lower than the lowest measured mark. They share a place and get 4 points each (decision 17: 4 for 4th and below).

## 4. Each round: call, throw, mark

What the teachers do: "Number 1, Mark Jones. Next, Andrew Johns." One official watches for fouls. The marking official puts the athlete's marker where the shot first lands, at the edge closest to the circle. On throws 2 and 3 the marker only moves if the throw is further than the athlete's best. If unsure, mark both.

In the app (phone, A1.19):
1. The screen shows the athlete, "No. 3 of 7", the weight, their 3 throw boxes, and Next: the next name.
2. After each throw, the recorder taps one button: **Fair** (landed inside the sector), **Foul**, or **Pass**.
3. A foul has no mark. The sheet's fouls (wrong technique, touching the top of the stop board or rim, leaving early or from the front, landing on or outside the lines) are all the same to the app.
4. A discus that hits the cage and lands inside the sector is Fair.
5. Wrong tap: Undo last.
6. No distance is typed during the throws. The marker stays on the ground, and only the best throw is measured, once, at the end (decision 34).

In the app (iPad, A1.18): the same, as a grid. Tap a box to step through Fair, Foul and Pass. Row: Throw 1, Throw 2, Throw 3, Best mark, Place, Points, No show.

App today: Fair, Foul, Pass, Undo and the grid are built.

## 5. Throw again, then measuring

**After throw 1 (Throw again):**
1. A Throw again screen lists every athlete with their throw 1 (Fair, Foul or Pass) and a tick box. The top shows "Throw again: 10" with the number editable, as a guide.
2. The official ticks the athletes who throw again. Everyone not ticked is marked Participation.
3. The official can still change the ticks before throw 2 starts, and Finish an athlete early at any time (it asks first and can be undone).

**At the end (measuring):**
1. The official measures the athletes they choose (about 5). The recorder types each mark on the keypad (534 is 5.34 m, nearest centimetre, decision 38). Only measured athletes are placed.
2. Everyone not measured is Participation. The official types one set distance for the group (for example 4.00 m). The app checks it is lower than the lowest measured mark.
3. Participation athletes share a place and get 4 points each. Equal measured marks share the place and points (decision 36). An athlete with no Fair throw at all, and a no-show, get 0 points.

App today: the phone keypad appears right after an athlete's 3rd throw, and the iPad Best mark box is always open. Both change (see "To draw").

## 6. Things that happen during the event

- **Athlete does not turn up or goes home:** tap No show. Their throws so far stay.
- **Late athlete after Start:** Add an athlete. The official decides how many throws they get (decision 75).
- **Marker moved by mistake or a ruling changes:** Undo last, or tap the throw and fix it. Places and points are worked out again.
- **Device dies or reloads:** it reopens on this station, this group, at the same athlete. Nothing typed is lost. Saves wait in a queue with no signal and show "Waiting to send" until sent.
- **Discus 1 and 2 are at opposite ends of the grounds, one device each. Shot Put's two groups at once each get their own device** (decision 83).

## 7. The end of the event

What the teachers do: mark 1st, 2nd and 3rd on the recording sheet and send it to the recording room.

In the app:
1. When the measured marks and the set participation distance are typed, tap Group finished.
2. The phone shows the group results to check (the A1.22 and A1.23 screen, decision 76): place, name, house, best mark, the throws as Fair, Foul or Pass, and points. Participation athletes show the set distance and "Participation". An athlete with no fair throw shows "No mark" and 0 points.
3. Check against what you saw, fix a throw with Back to throwing if needed, then tap Send results.
4. The Recorder and Announcer get it live. No paper is carried. After Send it shows "Sent at 10:52" and Next group. With no signal it says "Waiting to send" until it has gone.

App today: Group finished and Send are built. The phone results screen is drawn for high jump and long jump only.

## 8. Athletes leave, next group arrives

1. After Send, the screen opens the next group for this station and its Take Names screen, with the weight for that age.
2. Finished athletes go back to their house area, behind the safety area until called.
3. At the last group of the day, the screen says this station has no more groups.
