# High Jump: the day, from the station teacher's side

Updated 5 November 2026 (decisions 53 to 57): there is no Now up bar on the iPad (teachers tap the highlighted box and pick Clear, Miss or Pass), the start height and the raise are set by the person running the event, and a pass reads Pass. Where the steps below say Now up or a full-width raise step, the decisions win. The full-width raise step was not drawn.

Draft for Jordan, 5 November 2026. Source: `docs/2027 field event sheets/INSTRUCTIONS - High Jump.pdf`, decisions 27, 30, 35, 37, 40, 41 and Aths Opt 1.1 (PROJECT-STATE 2.3).

Who: two teachers at one station (High Jump 1 or High Jump 2), on one iPad or iPhone. One teacher calls and watches the bar. The other holds the device and records. One age group, boys or girls, at a time.

"App today" is what PROJECT-STATE says is built. It was not re-checked screen by screen for this note. "Change?" marks a gap between the sheet and the app, or a choice for Jordan. Nothing here is built or drawn yet.

## 1. Before the athletes arrive

What the teachers do: check the mat and the area. Set the bar at the starting height.

In the app:
1. Open the app, enter the Field Station PIN, pick the station (High Jump 1 or 2).
2. The screen shows the group for this block (for example "Boys 16 Years High Jump") and the starting height from the sheet (boys 1.20 m, girls 1.10 m for 15 to 18+; 13 and under 1.05 / 1.15; 14 years 1.10 / 1.15).
3. The teacher sets the bar to that height and checks it.

App today: station, group and starting height are held as organiser data.
Change? Show the starting height large on the Take Names screen, as the thing to set the bar to before anyone is called.

## 2. Athletes arrive: announce and take names

What the teachers do: announce "Boys 16 Years High Jump". Write each name and give a number in arrival order. This is the jumping order.

In the app (Take Names, A1.20):
1. As each athlete walks up, the recorder finds them in the list and taps the name. They get the next number, 1, 2, 3 and so on.
2. A second tap takes a name off and the numbers close up (a wrong tap, or someone who leaves).
3. "Not on the list" adds an athlete who is missing from the list.
4. When everyone is here, tap Start.

App today: all of this is built, on iPad and phone.
Change? Start should read the order back as a check ("Number 1 Mark Jones, Number 2 ...") so the caller can read it out. A late athlete after Start is covered in step 5.

## 3. Start: announce the first height

1. The screen shows the height, "Round 1", and Now up (Number 1) with Next (Number 2).
2. The caller announces the starting height.

App today: Now up and Next are built (A1.9).

## 4. Each round: call, jump, record, raise

What the teachers do: "Number 1, Mark Jones. Next, Andrew Johns." The athlete has 1 minute to start. Record the result while the bar is reset. Have the next athlete ready.

In the app, for the athlete who is up:
1. The caller reads Now up and Next from the screen.
2. The athlete jumps. The recorder taps one of:
   - **Clear**: the athlete clears the height. They are done at this height and wait for the next.
   - **Miss**: knocked the bar, two-foot take off, dived, ran past, or over 1 minute. This is try 1, 2 or 3 at this height.
   - **Pass**: the athlete skips this height. A pass can follow a miss and is shown X-. A pass does not reset the count of misses in a row.
3. The screen moves to the next athlete at once, so the bar can be reset while the next athlete gets ready.
4. A wrong tap: **Undo last**. A box on the grid can also be tapped to fix it.
5. Three misses in a row (a pass in between does not break the run) and the athlete goes out. Their box goes red when they go out on carried misses, and they drop out of the order.
6. When everyone still in has finished the height, the app works out the next height (decision 35):
   - Girls: +5 cm until 3 athletes remain, then +3 cm.
   - Boys: +10 cm for the first raise, then +5 cm until 3 remain, then +3 cm.
   - It shows "Next height 1.30 m" and the teacher raises the bar and announces it.

App today: Clear, Miss, Pass, Undo last, the try order (all 1st tries, then 2nds, then 3rds), 3 in a row, and the next height are built.
Change?
- Show the new height as a full-width "Raise the bar to 1.30 m (+5 cm)" step between rounds, so it is not missed while the athletes wait. The recorder taps Bar is set to carry on.
- The 1 minute rule: an optional 60 second countdown starting when Now up changes, going amber at 15 seconds. When it ends it does not record a miss; the recorder still taps Miss. Needs Jordan's OK (question 43).
- The bar checks (wind, sagging bar, official rulings) are the teachers' call. The app only records the result. Undo covers a changed ruling.

## 5. Things that happen during the event

- **Athlete does not turn up or goes home sick:** tap the name to take them off. Their earlier tries stay.
- **Late athlete (arrives after Start):** Add an athlete. Question 44: do they join at the current height with their earlier heights marked as not tried, or are they refused after Start?
- **Athlete wants to start higher than the set height:** this is Pass at every lower height. Check how it reads on the grid.
- **Mistake found a few rounds later:** tap the box on the grid and fix it. The places and ties are worked out again.
- **iPad dies or reloads:** the app reopens on this station, this group, at the same athlete. Nothing typed is lost. Saves wait in a queue with no signal and show "Waiting to send" until sent.
- **Both stations run at once:** each has its own device and its own group. They do not affect each other.

## 6. The end of the event

What the teachers do: the last athlete left keeps jumping until 3 misses in a row. Mark 1st, 2nd, 3rd. Send the sheet to the recording room with an athlete.

In the app:
1. The last athlete left keeps being Now up at each new height until 3 misses in a row. The app does not end the event while one athlete is still jumping. If only one athlete started, they jump until 3 misses.
2. When the last athlete is out, the recorder taps **Finish**.
3. Finish shows the result list: place, name, house, best height, misses at that height and overall. Ties are broken by fewer misses at the tied height, then fewer overall, then they share the place.
4. The teacher checks it against what they saw, fixes any box if needed, then taps **Send**.
5. The result goes to the Recorder and the Announcer live. There is no paper to carry, so no athlete is sent to the recording room.
6. A sent group can be reopened by the Carnival Organiser or Recorder if there was a mistake.

App today: Finish, tie rules, shared places and Send are built (decision 35, A1.9, A1.18 pattern).
Change? Tell the teacher what was sent ("Sent to Recorder, 10:52"), and show "Waiting to send" truthfully with no signal.

## 7. Athletes leave, next group arrives

1. After Send, the screen shows the next group for this station ("Girls 16 Years High Jump", 11:10) and its starting height.
2. The teachers reset the bar to the new starting height.
3. Athletes who have finished go back to their house area. The next group arrives and Take Names starts again at step 2.
4. At the last group of the day, the screen says the station has no more groups.

App today: the group list per block is held as organiser data; Send moves on.
Change? Make sure the next group opens on Take Names, not on the last group's result list.
